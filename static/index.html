How it works

  1. 01Code

    Input guard

    Strips hidden characters and flags anyone trying to trick the AI.

  2. 02AI

    Profiler

    Turns your words into a date, time, budget and physical needs.

  3. 03Code

    Course Locator

    Finds courses near your town or trip region and ranks them on measured hills, water and walking. Only the best ten reach the AI.

  4. 04AI

    Course Analyst

    Scores each course 0–100 for your group from the measurements. Unknown facts lower the score instead of being guessed.

  5. 05AI

    Concierge

    Picks the best courses (up to five for a trip) and tells you why, in plain words, with a booking link.

Honest about the data

Measured, not guessed. Course maps come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers have drawn most New England courses hole by hole. CaddieSync measures every hole against USGS elevation data: total climbing, the steepest stretch compared with the 8.33% ADA limit for a wheelchair ramp, shots over water, bunkers by each green, and the walk to the next tee.

Honest about gaps. Course rules (cart access near greens, adaptive carts) aren't on any map, so CaddieSync tells you exactly what to ask when you call. Price tiers ($ to $$$$) are researched for featured courses and marked unknown elsewhere. CaddieSync links you to each course's own booking page instead of guessing tee times.
