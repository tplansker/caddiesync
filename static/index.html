AI golf concierge

Tee times your whole group can actually play.

Tell CaddieSync who's playing and what's hard for them: a bad knee, no hills, a wheelchair, short drives. Three AI agents check every course's terrain, carts and hazards and find open tee times that fit.

How it works

  1. codeInput guard strips hidden characters and flags attack phrases.
  2. AIProfiler turns your words into a date, time, budget and physical needs.
  3. codeTee-Time Sniper scans tee sheets for open times that fit. Prices come only from here.
  4. AICourse Analyst scores each course 0–100 for your group. Scores are cached, so repeat questions are free.
  5. AIConcierge picks the best 1–3 and explains why, in plain words.

About the data

Course facts (terrain, cart rules, hazards, length) come from each course's own website, scorecard and public sources, with unknowns marked as unknown rather than guessed. Tee times and prices are simulated for this class project, since these courses don't offer a public tee-time API. Always confirm accessibility details with the course before you go.
